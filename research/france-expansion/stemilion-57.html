A word from the winegrower PHILIPPE FANIEST

Ideally located on the limestone plateau, Château Rochebelle is a family nugget.His superb monolith cellars contribute to the remarkable aging of his wines.Sleeping, the velvety of the tannins constitute the style of Rochebelle.
